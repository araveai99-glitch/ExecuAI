## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for senior executives, founders, and managing directors who evaluate risk, delegate rapidly, and require clarity under time pressure. The emotional tone is authoritative, composed, and discreetly intelligent. Rather than treating artificial intelligence as an autonomous black box or a playful chatbot, the interface positions the system as an impeccably organized digital chief of staff. The visual presentation evokes quiet confidence, stability, and unyielding privacy.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate Modern** with **Disciplined Minimalism**, inspired by high-density administrative interfaces and executive terminal workflows. Visual weight is carried by structured data tables, typographic scale, and purposeful whitespace rather than decorative illustrations or heavy glassmorphism. Artificial intelligence signals are restrained: probabilistic telemetry (such as raw numeric scores) is translated into clear, actionable executive summaries with visible safety gates and human-in-the-loop validation barriers.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate atmosphere of equilibrium and tactical focus. 

- **Primary Green (`#2E936F`)**: Anchor tone for verified safe actions, critical navigation anchors, and final human approvals. It communicates deliberate, verified progression without the aggressive urgency of saturated blues.
- **Secondary Slate (`#1E293B`)**: Serves as the executive grounding shade for high-level navigational chrome, primary headings, and definitive boundaries.
- **Tertiary Amber (`#FAB60A`)**: Signals mandatory human oversight, pending status reviews, and low-confidence drafts. It is never applied decoratively.
- **Primary Yellow (`#FFEC69`)**: Reserved strictly for contextual machine-intelligence indicators, email excerpt highlights, and proactive response recommendations. Its tinted surface counterpart (`#FFFDEB`) provides low-fatigue contrast for multi-paragraph synthesis.
- **Soft Beige (`#F7D7B0`)**: Confined to non-actionable administrative empty states, calm illustration foundations, and multi-tenant inbox separation markers.
- **Neutrals**: Built on a cold slate progression to maintain razor-sharp text legibility and defined 1px structural dividing lines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes `Inter` across all structural tiers to ensure uniform render performance, clear tabular numeral alignment, and low-cognitive-load scanning across executive dashboards. 

Headlines use tight negative tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.015em`) with deliberate line-heights that enforce clear visual boundaries between distinct operational sections. Body copy is tuned for prolonged document evaluation: email previews and message bodies rely on standard body scales (`body-md` and `body-lg`) with open line heights to maximize readability during rapid scanning. 

Monospaced numbers and metrics take advantage of Inter’s tabular figure settings (`tnum`), preventing layout jitter in live queue counts and processing metrics. Raw probability distributions and algorithmic metrics are strictly forbidden from the presentation layer; system logic is translated into concise verbal summaries (e.g., "High Risk — External wire instructions detected").

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses a fixed-fluid hybrid structure engineered for high-density information handling across four form factors:

- **Desktop (1280px - 1920px)**: A persistent 260px administrative navigation sidebar alongside a split pane: 420px fluid inbox column paired with an adaptive master reading/action surface (min-width 640px). 
- **Large Displays (2560px+)**: The application workspace caps at 1920px total width, horizontally centering on canvas with balanced outer margins to prevent extreme visual travel.
- **Tablet Landscape (1024px)**: An adaptive three-pane mode featuring a collapsible icon-only navigation rail (64px), condensed thread column (340px), and an active decision workbench.
- **Tablet Portrait (768px - 834px)**: Dual-pane drill-down layout with slide-out sidebar overlay.
- **Mobile (320px - 414px)**: Strict single-column stack with zero horizontal overflow. High-frequency actions and decision gates shift to a sticky bottom action bar (min-height 56px) configured to meet standard 44px minimum interactive touch zones.

Spacing leverages an 8px base grid rhythm (`0.5rem`). Internal component paddings strictly follow `space-sm` (8px) for badges and utility bars, `space-md` (16px) for item rows and standard card padding, and `space-xl` (32px) for canvas module demarcation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed primarily through crisp, low-contrast structural borders (`#E2E8F0`) and stepped neutral surface containers, rather than diffuse ambient shadows or layered glassmorphism.

1. **Canvas Surface (`#F8FAFC`)**: Base structural background across all window frames and column gutters.
2. **Elevated Panels (`#FFFFFF`)**: Email threads, AI synthesis cards, and configuration rows sit flat against the canvas, delineated by a solid `1px` border in `#E2E8F0`. Hover states on interactive list items do not elevate; they shift background color to `#F1F5F9`.
3. **Floating Contextual Overlays (Popovers, Dropdowns)**: Elevated with a clinical, subtle boundary shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` wrapped in a `1px` border of `#CBD5E1`.
4. **Safety & Decision Modals (Approval Gates, Sensitive Dispatch)**: Positioned above an opaque, non-blurred backdrop overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`). The modal card uses a focused elevation: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture is built on a **Soft (`1`)** corner radius standard, reinforcing precision and functional utility without looking sterile or industrial.

- Standard interactive controls, form fields, cards, and modal sheets carry a `0.25rem` (4px) or `0.375rem` (6px) radius.
- Structural panels, multi-pane dividers, and dashboard layout cards feature `0.5rem` (8px) corners (`rounded-lg`).
- Status pills, priority tags, and mailbox origin indicators use full-radius pill styling (`9999px`) to maintain sharp contrast against rectangular card layouts and data grid borders.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Approve / Execute)**: Solid `#2E936F` fill, white text, 6px border radius, 40px height (`py-2 px-4`), Inter Medium. Focus state: 2px offset ring with `#2E936F`.
- **Secondary (Edit Draft / Re-analyze)**: Neutral outline. `#FFFFFF` background, `1px` solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#1E293B`. Hover: `#F8FAFC`.
- **Hazard Gate (Reject / Mark High Risk)**: Subtle crimson surface (`#FFF1F2`), text `#E11D48`, border `1px` solid `#FECDD3`. Hover: `#FFE4E6`.
- **Touch Target**: Touch areas on mobile automatically expand to 44px minimum via invisible negative margins.

### Classification & Status Badges
Every email component separates attributes into three distinct, non-overlapping dimensions:
- **Priority**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `py-0.5 px-2.5`, label-xs font.
  - *Critical/Urgent*: Light red fill (`#FFE4E6`), dark red text (`#9F1239`).
  - *Important/Normal*: Slate fill (`#F1F5F9`), slate text (`#334155`).
- **Intent**: Rectangular soft corners (`rounded-sm`), neutral background, `1px` border `#E2E8F0`, uppercase tracking (`Client`, `Vendor`, `Internal`, `Legal`).
- **Risk Gate**:
  - *Safe*: Soft green outline, `#2E936F` text.
  - *Review Required*: Solid `#FAB60A` text, warning surface (`#FEF7E6`), `1px` solid `#FDE68A`.
  - *High Risk*: Solid crimson indicator badge. Disables one-click dispatch triggers automatically.

### Multi-Mailbox Identity Indicators
To guarantee multi-tenant clarity, each thread contains an origin indicator at the extreme left:
- **Google Workspace**: Clean minimal `G` indicator with slate tag border.
- **Zoho Mail**: Clean minimal `Z` indicator with slate tag border.
- Indicators maintain identical height (20px) and font specs to prevent visual baseline displacement.

### Form Inputs & Executive Prompts
- Text areas and quick-instruction inputs use `#FFFFFF` background, `1px` solid `#CBD5E1`, internal padding `space-sm space-md`.
- Focus states suppress default browser outlines in favor of a crisp `2px` ring in `#2E936F` with zero blur offset.

### Intelligence Highlight Cards
- Contextual suggestions, message summaries, and drafted responses render inside dedicated intelligence modules featuring a subtle yellow-tinted background (`#FFFDEB`) framed with an accent left border (`3px solid #FAB60A`). Extracted quotes or critical decisions within thread views use an inline highlight of `#FFEC69` at 40% alpha.
